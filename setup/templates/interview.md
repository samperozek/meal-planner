# Setup interview: question bank

Ask in six rounds of up to four questions each. Put the recommended option first, and let people type their own answer. Skip anything already answered. After each round, write what you learned to `my-recipes/PROFILE.md`; it becomes the brief every research agent reads.

## Round 1: Household
1. **Who eats dinner from the plan?** (e.g. "2 adults + 2 kids", "just me", "couple"). Ask the kids' ages; they set portion sizes.
2. **How many dinner servings should a typical recipe make?** Default 4. Young children together often eat about one adult portion.
3. **Who eats breakfast and lunch from the plan?** Just the cook (batch-prepped), the whole family, or nobody (dinners only).
4. **Leftovers:** do you like them for lunches or a second dinner, or not at all?

## Round 2: Diet and health
1. **Allergies or intolerances?** These are absolute exclusions.
2. **Eating style:** none, vegetarian, pescatarian, vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-FODMAP…
3. **Health goals:** high-protein breakfast and lunch (and the grams per meal), lighter weeknights, limit red meat or dairy (say how much), lower sodium, lower carb, none.
4. **Strong dislikes:** ingredients nobody will eat (cilantro, mushrooms, olives, seafood…).

## Round 3: Tastes
1. **Favorite cuisines** (choose several): Italian, Mexican, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Indian, Korean, Middle Eastern, Greek and Mediterranean, French, American comfort, Southern and Cajun, Caribbean…
2. **A heritage or home cuisine** you want represented (a few dishes, not the whole collection).
3. **Spice tolerance:** mild for kids, medium, hot.
4. **Adventurousness:** mostly familiar favorites, a mix, or new things weekly.

## Round 4: Time and effort
1. **Most dinners you want to cook a week.** Default 5, with one rest night and one leftovers night.
2. **Weeknight hands-on cap:** 15, 20 or 30 minutes.
3. **Weekend appetite:** one longer project, an occasional project, or keep weekends easy too.
4. **Equipment:** slow cooker, Instant Pot, grill, smoker, air fryer, stand mixer, Dutch oven, sheet pans, cast iron.

## Round 5: Skills and growth
1. **Current skill:** beginner, comfortable, confident, advanced.
2. **Goal:** get dinner on the table, eat healthier, cook more variety, or become an excellent cook.
3. **Culinary school?** A weekly lesson that teaches one technique through a classic dish, in a deliberate sequence. Weekly, every other week, later, or no.
4. **If yes, the focus:** classic French technique, global kitchens, baking and pastry, grilling and smoking, or a mix.

## Round 6: Logistics
1. **Grocery store:** Amazon Whole Foods (cart filling is built for it), another online store (the planner gives you the list; filling the cart may need adapting), or no delivery (list only).
2. **Orders a week:** one (a Sunday delivery) or two (Sunday plus a Thursday top-up for the end of the week, so skipped meals don't waste food). Budget target, if any.
3. **Texts:** your own phone number for iMessage (Mac only), or no texts.
4. **Location** (city, for weather) and **calendars** to read for busy nights (and which to ignore).

## Read it back
Summarize in 8–12 lines (household, diet rules, cuisines, time limits, equipment, growth goals, logistics) and get a clear yes before Phase 2.
